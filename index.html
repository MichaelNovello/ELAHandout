Course Overview

Sixth grade Language Arts (course M/J Language Arts 1) is built around the state's B.E.S.T. English Language Arts standards. The course focuses on building a wide range of knowledge across subjects, since that knowledge base is seen as essential for strong literacy skills. Students also start developing their command of rhetoric — the craft of using language effectively in writing and speaking — by studying classic literature, essays, and speeches as models. About a third of the literature used in class comes from the state's official 6th Grade Sample Book List.

By the end of the year, students are expected to master a defined set of benchmarks, while continuing to reinforce skills from earlier grades. Florida treats English Language Arts as a rich, meaningful subject rather than just a checklist of isolated skills — the goal is for students to become confident readers, writers, and communicators who can fully participate in society.

Supplies, Requirements, and Recommendations

Students should have the following items with them each day:

You will keep your supplies with you — they are not stored in the classroom.

  • A plastic folder with clips and pockets (any color)
    • You need a place to keep your papers and handouts
  • Pencils (no mechanical pencils please)
  • Pens (blue or black only) — please don't do your work in any other color
  • Pencil sharpener — hand held, with shaving catcher
  • Highlighters (yellow, blue, and green)
  • A book to read — always have a book to read!
  • Paper — college rule paper — this does not need to be in a spiral or composition notebook
  • Colored pencils, markers, or crayons — you will need these throughout the year
  • You might want to invest in a pair of plug-in earbuds (not Bluetooth or wireless)

You should NEVER come to class empty handed. Always have your materials with you. Always.

Textbook: StudySync

StudySync is your textbook this year. Since it is a workbook, students will be writing directly in the book.

  • You should NOT rip out pages
  • You should NOT draw on the cover
  • You should NOT do anything to deface the book

All students will have digital access to the curriculum this year.
